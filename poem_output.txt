In Aalborg, clouds conceal the sky,
A gloomy veil, with winds that sigh.
Beijing, on the other hand, shines bright,
With gentle breeze and warm, sunny light.
Nanning's humidity, a sweltering weight,
A heavy mist, that clings to every fate.
But Beijing's charm, is where I'd choose to be,
With clear skies, and warmth, wild and free.
The city's beauty, on this day will shine,
A perfect blend, of sun and gentle wind's design.
