As morning dawns on Aalborg's shore,
A gentle breeze stirs, with clouds galore.
Beijing, in contrast, shines so bright,
With warmer temps and a gentle, sweet delight.
Nanning's skies are grey, with rain's dark might,

Beijing's warmth and sunshine banish all fear.
