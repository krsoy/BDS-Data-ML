🌍 English
Tomorrow's skies, a tale of three cities told,
Aalborg's gray, with clouds that unfold,
Beijing shines, with sunbeams bright and bold,
A warm haven, where hearts can unfold.
Nanning's veil, a humid, cloudy shroud,
Conceals the sun, in a misty crowd,
Beijing's breeze, a gentle, whispering sigh,
Invites the soul, to dance, and touch the sky.
The wind in Aalborg, a fierce, wild cry,
Contrasts with Beijing's, gentle, summer sky,
Beijing's charm, a balance, of warmth and light,
Makes it the nicest, to bask in its delight.
