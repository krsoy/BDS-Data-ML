Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's clouds, a somber guise,
Beijing's sun, a warm delight,
Nanning's storms, a rainy night.
Beijing's breeze, a gentle caress,
Aalborg's wind, a chilly mess,
Nanning's humidity, a sweltering haze,
Beijing's the place, where sunshine stays.
The temperature's just right, not too high,
A pleasant day, with a clear blue sky.
So if you could choose, where to be tomorrow,
Beijing's the city, with the most pleasant weather to borrow.
