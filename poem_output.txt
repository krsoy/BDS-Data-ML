🌍 English
In Aalborg's chill, the winds do blow,
A cloudyless sky, yet humidity's woe.
Beijing's veil, a misty, rainy shroud,
Heavy with cloud, and precipitation allowed.
Nanning's warmth, a gentle, sunny breeze,
A cloudless sky, with humidity's soft ease.
The choice is clear, where sunshine doth abide,
Nanning's charm, will be the nicest place to reside.
The temperature sweet, the winds a gentle hush,
A perfect day, in Nanning's lovely rush.
