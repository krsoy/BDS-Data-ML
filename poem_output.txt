Tomorrow's skies, a tale of three cities tell,
Aalborg's grey, with clouds that heavily dwell,
Beijing's warmth, a gentle breeze does bring,
A soothing calm, with sunshine's joyful ring.
Nanning's clear, with not a cloud in sight,
But Aalborg's chill, and Beijing's delight,

With mild winds, and a pleasant humidity.
The rain probability, a low five percent chance,
Makes Beijing the winner, in this weather dance.
