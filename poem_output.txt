🌍 English
Tomorrow's skies, a tale of three,
Aalborg's chill, with winds that freeze,
Beijing's haze, a cloudy sea,
Nanning's heat, with storms to be.
But of the three, one stands apart,
Beijing's warmth, a gentle heart,
No rain to fall, no winds to bite,
A pleasant day, a pure delight.
The clouds may hide, the sun's bright face,
But in Beijing, a peaceful place,
The humidity, a gentle breeze,
Invites you to stroll, with ease.
