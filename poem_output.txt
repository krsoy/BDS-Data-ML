🌍 English
Three cities, different skies, yet all grey,
Aalborg's chill, Beijing's heat, in disarray.
Aalborg's winds howl strong, with clouds so bright,
Beijing's rain threatens, with a humid night.
Nanning's calm, with warmth, and clouds that sleep,
A gentle breeze, that whispers soft and deep.
The humidity's high, but the sun's gentle rays,
Make Nanning the haven, for a peaceful day.
The other two, with winds and rain, can't compare,
To Nanning's serenity, beyond compare.
