🌍 English
In Aalborg's chill, the winds do blow,
A chilly 14 degrees, with skies of woe.
Beijing's heat, a different tale unfolds,
28 degrees of warmth, with clouds that hold.
Nanning's mist, a humid, cloudy veil,
With rain's sweet promise, and a gentle gale.
But Beijing's charm, with sunshine in its heart,
Makes it the nicest place to play its part.
The gentle breeze, the warm and cloudy sky,
Invite you to Beijing, to wander and sigh.
