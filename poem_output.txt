Tomorrow's skies, a varied hue,
Aalborg's gentle breeze, with clouds anew.
Beijing's warmth, a sunny delight,
A haven from the cold, with gentle night.
Nanning's heat, with clouds that gather fast,
A stormy forecast, that may not last.
But if I had to choose, where to roam,
Beijing's charm, would be my heart's home.
The gentle wind, the sunshine bright,
Make it the perfect place, for a peaceful night.
The contrast is stark, between the three,
But Beijing's beauty, is the one for me.
