🌍 English
Three cities, different skies, a tale to tell,
Aalborg's grey, with winds that loudly yell.
Beijing's warmth, a gentle summer breeze,
Invites the heart, with sunny memories.
Nanning's clouds, a deep and mystic sea,
Shroud the city, in humidity.
But Beijing's balance, of sun and gentle rain,
Makes it the haven, where love and joy remain.
The temperature's sweet, the winds are low and sweet,
A perfect day, to find a love to greet.
