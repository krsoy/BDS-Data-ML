🌍 English
Tomorrow's skies, a tale of three,
Aalborg's chill, with winds that spree,
Beijing's calm, with sunshine bright,
A gentle breeze, a wondrous sight.
Nanning's heat, with clouds so low,
A chance of rain, the winds do blow.
But if I had to choose a place to be,
Beijing's serenity, is where I'd be.
The warmth and gentle wind, a perfect blend,
A day to stroll, with heart and soul to mend.
